Be honest with yourself

Which one is right for you?

Pick PocketFi if
  • You’re on Android (or happy to wait a few months for iOS).
  • You want shared wallets that actually work — partner, roommates, trips.
  • You’d rather scan a receipt than type one.
  • You care that no one is selling your data.
  • You like that you could read every line of code if you wanted to.
  • You want it to be free — not free-with-ads-and-a-paywall.
Maybe pick something else if
  • You absolutely need bank sync today (try Wallet by BudgetBakers).
  • You’re iOS-only and can’t wait until Q3 2026.
  • You want hundreds of custom categories (try Money Lover).
  • You’d rather build it yourself in Notion or Google Sheets. (Genuinely, no shade.)
Switching over?

We’ll help you migrate.

Most apps let you export CSV. Send it to us and we’ll import it into your PocketFi account for you — free, no questions, while we build the in-app importer (Q4 2026).
