Everything in PocketFi.

The four pillars that hold the app up, the 17 categories underneath, and the smaller pieces that quietly make it work.

photo_cameraReceipt OCR

Snap, don’t type.

Point your camera at any receipt. PocketFi reads the merchant, amount, date, and even line items — then drops you into the edit screen with everything pre-filled. The OCR runs on-device via Google ML Kit, so the image never leaves your phone for processing.

  • check_circleOn-device ML Kit — no upload
  • check_circleMerchant + amount + date auto-filled
  • check_circleAttach the receipt image to the entry
  • check_circleWorks offline
peopleShared wallets

Split it, track it, done.

Pool a wallet with up to 8 members. Every entry is visible to the group, every contribution is tallied, every "wait, who paid for dinner?" question becomes a glance. Personal wallets stay private — shared and personal never bleed into each other.

  • check_circleUp to 8 members per wallet
  • check_circlePer-member contribution view
  • check_circleEven, percentage, or custom splits
  • check_circleEmail invitations, no phone numbers required
donut_largeMonthly overview

See where it went.

A donut for the month’s spending, a ranked category list, and a nudge when something’s drifting. Tap any slice to drill into the entries. Pinned (bookmarked) categories rank first, so the things you care about stay at the top.

  • check_circleAnimated donut + category ranking
  • check_circleCompare month-over-month
  • check_circleSet per-category monthly budgets
  • check_circleGentle overspend warnings
list_altTimeline

Every entry, in order.

Scroll the day-by-day list of everything you’ve recorded. Filter by wallet, category, or tag. Search by merchant. Tap any entry to edit it; long-press to bookmark for one-tap re-entry.

  • check_circleFilter by wallet, category, or tag
  • check_circleSearch by merchant
  • check_circleOne-tap re-entry for bookmarked merchants
  • check_circleBulk edit + bulk delete
