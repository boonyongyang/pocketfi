What we're building next.

How this works

Plans change. We'll tell you when they do.

This is an honest plan, not a contract. If something slips, we'll mark the issue and the next changelog will explain why. If you really need a feature, the fastest path is to +1 the issue or open a PR.

Not on the roadmap

Things we won't add.

  • Ads. Ever.
  • Selling anonymized data.
  • Crypto / NFT integrations.
  • AI chatbot for "financial advice".
  • A premium tier — PocketFi stays free.
