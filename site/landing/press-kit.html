One-liner
PocketFi is a free, open-source budget tracker for receipts, shared wallets, and the question of where MYR 200 went last week.
Tagline
Track your ringgit, simply.
50 words
PocketFi is a Flutter-built budget tracker for everyday spending. Snap a receipt, the OCR fills in the merchant and amount. Pool a wallet with roommates or your partner. See where your month went on a clean monthly overview. Free, publicly documented, and community-trackable. Release preparation in progress.
